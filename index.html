About the Bristol scale

Types 1–2 suggest constipation, 3–4 are typical, and 5–7 lean toward diarrhea.

This app is a personal log, not medical advice. Contact your care team for heavy bleeding, black/tarry stool, fever, severe or worsening pain, or signs of dehydration.
